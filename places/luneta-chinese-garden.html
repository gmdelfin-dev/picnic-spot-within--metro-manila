Luneta Chinese Garden

You can find more information about luneta chinese garden here:

https://npdc.gov.ph/

I recommend this place for anyone who wants to enjoy a picnic while experiencing a Chinese inspired atmosphere. Its peaceful garden and traditional surroundings make it a pleasant place to enjoy food, take a quiet walk, and spend time with family or friends.

Note: The opening hours may vary depending on the season and special events. It is advisable to check the official website or contact the management for the most up-to-date information.
