A gentle reminders my friend:

  • Bring water and snacks for both you and your pets, if you have any.
  • Bring a picnic mat or something comfortable to sit on.
  • Bring something that lets you slow down, like a sketchbook, journal, knitting materials, or anything you enjoy.
  • Be mindful of your surroundings and keep an eye on your belongings.
  • Check the weather before heading out.
  • Keep the area clean and respect the space and other people.

Take a moment to slow down and simply enjoy where you are.
