Glayza Mae S. Delfin

Allow me to show you lovely corners of Metro Manila, where you can slow down and rest.


Why I Chose This Topic?

I chose this topic because I realized how stressful life can be, especially when we are constantly worrying about school, workload, responsibilities, and the many things we need to accomplish. Living in Metro Manila can sometimes make it even more tiring because of the traffic, crowded streets, and the busy surroundings that we experience almost every day. I created this guide to share places where young people and young adults can have a picnic, spend time with someone they care about, or simply have some time for themselves away from their usual routines. I wanted to show that taking a break does not always have to mean going somewhere far or spending a lot, because sometimes a simple afternoon outdoors can already give us the chance to breathe and clear our minds. Through these places, I hope people can slow down for a while, appreciate nature and their surroundings, and remember to enjoy the little moments that often get overlooked in our busy lives.


What Makes My Recommendations Worth Trusting?

I chose these places based on their location, surroundings, and the kind of experience they can offer to someone who is looking for a place to rest, relax, and spend time outdoors. Some of these recommendations also come from my own experiences, while others are based on the experiences and suggestions of people I know who have visited these places. I wanted to include places that I believe can provide a comfortable and enjoyable experience, while also giving people different options depending on what kind of environment they prefer. These recommendations are not meant to be the only good places to visit, but rather a starting point for anyone looking for a peaceful spot within Metro Manila.
